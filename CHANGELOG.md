# Changelog

## 0.1.0

Initial release.

- Agents restate your design and model it; TLA+ and Alloy search every state the
  models allow, within stated bounds, for one where a claim fails.
- You confirm the restatement and rule on each fault, shown as steps in your own
  terms. What holds is computed from the runs and your decisions.
- The `forma`, `forma-explorer` and `forma-toolchain` commands, from the npm
  package `@methodologies/forma`. The pinned toolchain is fetched once.
